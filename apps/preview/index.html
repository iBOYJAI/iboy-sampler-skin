A VIDEO.JS V10 SKIN

Sampler

A player you can sew. Pause the film and a needle stitches the frame back in as cross-stitch. Play it and a machine sews a seam, every stitch as tall as the sound was loud.

FOOTAGE
FABRIC

Press play, then pause. Drag the needle along the seam. Try other footage and other fabrics.

01 CROSS-STITCH

The frame is re-stitched

Pause, and a needle sews the frozen frame back in, one cross at a time, in 36 thread colours on aida cloth. Cells close to the cloth are left bare, as a stitcher would. The poster before play is a finished sampler, and pressing play unpicks it.

02 ZIGZAG

The seam is the sound

The seek bar is a chalked line. As the film plays, a zigzag is sewn along it, and each stitch is as tall as that moment was loud. Nothing is pre-analysed, and the audio is only read, never rerouted.

03 RIVET AND BUTTON

Play is a rivet

Play is a domed copper rivet. Every other control is a sewn button, and the icons are redrawn as running stitches, so even the line art is made of thread.

04 TOPSTITCH

Denim you can read on

The controls sit on a denim panel with double gold topstitching, so they stay legible over any footage. The title is a leather patch, the time is a typewritten care label, and tooltips are hang tags.

MADE IN TIRUPPUR

iBOY's studio is in Tiruppur, Tamil Nadu, a town built on knitwear. Sampler borrows its workshop.

SIZES

One garment, every width

The controls follow the width of the player itself, not the window. Narrow players drop the title and the cast and picture-in-picture buttons, hand the centre cluster over to the panel, and keep every button thumb sized.

ACCESS

Made for everyone

  • Readable over any footage

    Controls sit on a solid denim panel, never on the picture. Light text on Indigo denim is above 10 to 1.

  • Keyboard first

    Every control is reachable in visual order, with a thick gold focus ring. The shortcuts are below.

  • Thumb sized

    Buttons are 44 px on touch screens and never smaller than 40 px elsewhere.

  • Calm on request

    With reduced motion on, the needle does not travel: the frame appears stitched, and the seam redraws only a few times a second.

  • Forced colours and contrast

    High contrast and forced colours modes swap the fabric for solid system colours.

  • Never blocks playback

    The seam reads a copy of the audio and never routes it. If a browser or file cannot provide one, an even seam is sewn instead.

KEYBOARD

  • Space or K play and pause
  • J L or ← → seek
  • ↑ ↓ volume
  • M mute
  • F fullscreen
  • C captions
  • I picture in picture
  • 0 to 9 jump to a point

USE IT

Open source, Apache 2.0

Sampler is an ejected Video.js v10 HTML skin: markup, one stylesheet and two small custom elements. It takes the same public properties as the packaged skins.

data-sampler-theme
indigo, black, stone or canvas
--media-accent-color
thread colour, default gold #e8a33d
--media-border-radius
player corners, default 20px
--media-font-family
interface font, default Poppins
